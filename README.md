# Moist-Me-Up

Moist-Me-Up is a playful, interactive digital lip-care mobile application designed to gamify hydration. When your real-life lips feel chapped, you open the app to apply moisturizing products to your customized digital lips. Starting with classic Vaseline, the more you apply and look after your digital lips, the more premium lip products, glosses, and balms you unlock.

---

## Project Overview

The core philosophy of Moist-Me-Up is to turn a mundane daily habit (applying lip balm) into a digital experience. 

### Key Features
* **Customized Personalization:** Users select their gender, lip shape, and lip color to match their real-world self.
* **Gamified Hydration:** A swipe-to-apply mechanic that updates your digital lips from chapped to glossy.
* **Progression System:** Start with basic Vaseline and earn experience points (XP) or streak milestones to unlock premium lip care products.
* **Product Collection:** A virtual shelf displaying all unlocked and locked lip products.

---

## Mobile App Screens and Architecture

The Figma file contains 10 fully editable mobile screens mapped out under the App Artboards page, structured as follows:

### 1. Authentication Flow
* **Welcome Screen:** Splash landing with playful branding and entry buttons.
* **Sign Up:** Minimalist user registration form.
* **Login:** Quick access portal for returning users.

### 2. Personalization and Onboarding
* **Gender Selection:** Accessible options to establish the user avatar baseline.
* **Lip Customization:** Interactive setup to choose specific lip shapes and natural lip colors.

### 3. Core Interaction (The Main Hub)
* **Digital Lips Canvas (Vaseline Stage):** The main interface displaying chapped digital lips with a prominent swipe-to-apply mechanic utilizing starter Vaseline.
* **Progress and Metrics:** Real-time feedback showing current lip hydration levels, application streaks, and XP tracking.

### 4. Collection and Progression
* **Product Unlocks:** Celebration overlay screens triggered when a milestone is met and a new product is discovered.
* **Product Collection:** A catalog or shelf layout showcasing currently owned products (such as Vaseline, flavored balms, tinted glosses) alongside silhouetted locked items.

### 5. Account Management
* **Profile Screen:** Summary of user stats, total lifetime applications, unlocked tiers, and basic settings.
